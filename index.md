I build tools and protocols that help people coordinate, collaborate and understand each other.

The reason is simple to state. Most of the big problems we face are coordination failures. We know a lot, we can do a lot, and we still can't act together well enough. I think better tools for thinking together are part of the answer, and I would rather build them in public than keep notes to myself.

## What's here

- **[Seed spec](./seed-spec.md)**: a small format for publishing how a personal AI system actually runs, so other people's agents can take ideas from it safely. [Format](https://github.com/sharky-fm/seed-spec) · [my system, written in it](https://github.com/sharky-fm/seed-spec-personal-ai-system).
- **The commitment**: keep an up-to-date, public description of the tools I use and build, and a list of people I recommend who do the same. I'd like that to become a normal thing to do.

More will land here as it gets built: notes on collaboration tools, thinking-together protocols, and what running a personal AI for real teaches you.
